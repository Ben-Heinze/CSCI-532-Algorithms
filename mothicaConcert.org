I went to a Mothica concert on 3/28/2026! The lineup wad Diamante, Until I Wake, Mothica, then I See Stars. It was such an amazing time. Before I headed to Billings, I was taking a shower and thought, "You know what would be fun? Making a resume for a moth since Mothica is in her "Mothicorp" era. So instead of driving to BIllings at noon like I originally had planned, I spent an hour and a half drawing a suit on a moth I found online and researching what moths would say when applying for a job.

Once I finished, I called up Wadhams who lives across the street to see if I could use his printer. It was good timing since he was about to go on a camping trip with Clara. I print two copies, then head to Billing a little before 2pm. I check into my Dude Rancher Lodge hotel(?) room (weird name but the place had a fun Western personality to it without being cringey), and called Tony on the phone. Mothica said she sometimes goes to the merch table right after her show to say hi to fans, (the main reason I made the moth resume) but she posted a story on her instagram saying she needed to rest her voice instead. Totally understandable, the Spin The Wheel tour was BRUTAL. Despite that, I figured I'd bring one of my resume copies . This was my first time goint to a concert alone so I figured Id just show up once the doors upened at 7 instead of getting early to be the first to enter like I typically did in the past. There was a very short line, I get to the Pub Station and first thing I do is check out the merch tables, which where directly in front of the doors as you entered the venue. There was only a couple people in front of me so I internally argued what I should and shouldn't buy. Once it was my turn, I told the nice merch lady, "Hello, may I please get I'd like a large sweatshiii- oh my god is that a drum head?". The other customers blocked my view of it, but there was this lightly used drumhead with a big moth drawing with stars floating around it. It had the date, and two signatures: Mothica's and the drummer she is touring with, Carter Vaughan. It was only $100... Obviously I had to get it.

[insert picture of drum head here]

The merch lady said it was the only one. They aren't guaranteed at every show and she'll only draw on one if they can spare it and if she's feeling it. As the merch stand lady was ringing my order up, we had a little conversation about the tour aand I asked if she was a fan, she gave me smile and said yes! There was nobody in line after me so I wasn't holding anyone up, so I figured she would get a laugh out of the moth resume I made. I pull the folded up piece of paper out of my pocket and hand it to her. She laughed at it and offered to give it to Mothica! I wasn't expecting that at all, but I said thatd be super nice of her! I officially felt accomplished at this concert! I put on the sweatshirt and carried the drumhead around me.

It wasn't soon after until Diamante started her set. I sat in the semi-back right of the showroom for her and Until I Wake. I listened to a couple of her songs when I first heard about this tour, 1987 being the only one I remembered, and I enjoyed her sound on spotify as a whole but didn't give her enough time to become a consistent artist (same with the other bands). The crowd was a little weak with her, as most openers suffer from, but her vocals were very impressive. She can definitely belt. The guitarists were rocking some awesome solos too. I enjoyed her show and I was trying to be interactive by throwing my empty hand up for most of it. After her set she went to her merch stand to take photos and say hi. There was a long line and I got in it for a second, but Until I Wake started playing and the line didn't move very fast; Id rather see the start of the next set.

Until I Wake is more of a half screamo band. SUPER GOOD! You can tell that most of the artists voices were strained (again, BRUTAL tour) but the lead singer had his screams ON POINT. I need to check them out. Their tour bus driver got sick so the band members have to take shifts driving the bus. They also went to their merch stand after, but I needed to see mothica. They had pit form a few times during their songs, and a big one to end their set with. This was the perfect time to sneak from the back to the front. I was basically second row, with one person truly in front of me and I could see over her head no problem. Perfect view, basically front row. As the time comes, She starts toxic, her touring guitarist Alex tells us that that he had the best burger of his live at the Burger Dive (huge compliment since hes probably had burgers in every state), and played a great set. She also had a strained voice but she was giving the crowd a great show.  she did a really cool half-slowed/half faster version of Forever Fifteen after giving a speech about how she tried to end her life, that was a teary-eyed moment for me lol. She plays another song, and the something I never expected happened: She says this has never happened before, then PULLS OUT THE RESUME I QUICKLY MADE ON STAGE!!! I was to hyped. After explaining to the crowd what Mothicorp is, she read the name, said that he was a night shift pollination specialist, and read the part that heis a top Mothica fan with one of his favorite songs being TEARS, and how he circles porchlights as a Hobby! She loved it, I was so happy. I was in the front row so I yelled, "Thats mine!!!". Mothica looks through the crowd after hearing, that, points me out looking me in the eye and said, "You made this?". I start nodding my head and I hold up the drumhead she drew earlier to show Im a big fan. Life. Fulfilled. Amazing. astonishing. I can't believe she pointed me out to the crowd because of some silly idea I had an hour and a half before I was supposed to drive over to Billings. One of the photographers behind the barrier gestured me to hold the drum head up again, and the concert continued. This was so much better than I could have expected. I was originally a little bummed since she wasn't going to go to her merch stand after playing, but I got an autograph (way cooler than on a CD), Mothica got to see the resume I made (annd i realized a couple mistakes in it after, this will haunt me forever), AND SHE INTERACTED WITH ME!! This was the best possible outcome. The rest of the concert was amazing, I was jumping up and down screaming the lyrics to all her songs. I saw on the setlist that she's played an unreleased song called PLAYING-GOD on other tours, but unfortunately she didn't play it today. Last couple of songs were VICES and Casualty. Amazing. 10/10. Would go again.

After her set, I leave the front row to make room for bigger I See Stars fans and I grabbed a beer (Goose IPA). One guy came up to me and said congrats and had a brief conversation with him, then a couple guys in front of us overheard and turned around after the first guy left. One of the guys walks away. The guy I met was named robert, he is born and raised in Billings, and was here because he works at the Pub Station and likes to see shows (doesnt get in for free though). He called himself a poser, and I was like nah youre just exapanding your music taste. We laughed about it, then he told me how to started working in the kitchen here and took some shifts covering for people at the merch stand. He said the metal shows are people cosplaying as mean people, while the pop-ier fans are mean people cosplaying as nice people, haha. We had a good laugh and went on our way.
